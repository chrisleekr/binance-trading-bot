# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

This is a small project maintained by one person in their spare time. Nobody is owed a fix, a review, or a reply on any timeline.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Demanding, entitled, or abusive behaviour toward the maintainer or other contributors.
- Deliberately posting someone else's credentials, or filing reports engineered to get others to leak their own.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit messages in this repository.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, lock, or delete any contribution and block any account, at their discretion, without prior warning. There is no appeals process.

To report a problem, use GitHub's [report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow. For a security vulnerability, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.
